About Sarvee Dental Hospital

Located in the heart of Konkapalli, Amalapuram, Sarvee Dental Hospital is a premier dental healthcare facility dedicated to providing clean, modern, and patient-first care. We combine advanced diagnostic tools with personalized treatment plans to ensure you receive the highest standard of dental therapy in a relaxing and sterile environment.

Whether you need simple preventive teeth maintenance or advanced cosmetic and implant procedures, our specialists are dedicated to your comfort and absolute satisfaction.

Our Expertise

Treatments & Services

From smile design to implants and fixed teeth, every treatment is planned with care and precision.

Smile Design

Advanced aesthetic procedures to reshape and brighten your teeth for a perfect, natural smile.

Dental Implants

State-of-the-art permanent root replacements to securely mount natural-looking fixed teeth.

Dentures

Premium quality complete and partial removable dentures designed for optimal comfort and function.

Teeth Cleaning & Polishing

Professional scaling and stain removal to keep your gums healthy and your teeth shiny white.

Fillings & Restorations

Biocompatible, tooth-colored materials to repair cavities and restore damaged teeth seamlessly.

Fixed Teeth / Crowns & Bridges

Durable zirconia and ceramic crowns to protect teeth and bridges to fill gaps permanently.
